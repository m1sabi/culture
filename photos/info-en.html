What is MK-PH for?

MK-PH sends selected photos and videos directly from your Android phone to a private SMB share on your home computer or H-Cloud . Your phone must reach the share over home Wi-Fi or a home VPN. No Windows upload script or PC browser window is needed.

Each phone saves uploads in its own backup folder and groups them by capture date. The gallery does not show the backup folder location.

The normal date-sorted gallery shows this phone's photos and videos that MK-PH recorded as uploaded. It reads their local copies for thumbnails and viewing, so returning to the gallery does not rescan the share. A shared album link can show photos chosen by another household phone. Albums organize references to these files; they do not rearrange the originals on your phone or move them on the share.

Get started on a new phone

  1. Connect to your home Wi-Fi. Enter the server, share, SMB account and SMB account password supplied by the owner. Give this phone its own folder name, such as A52s.
  2. Enter your owner-approved email ID and its assigned activation code once. The owner sends the code separately; MK-PH does not send email or contact an internet service. The SMB password and activation code are different. The code is required when activating a new installation; it is not requested each time you open the app.
  3. Follow the on-screen setup guide: choose All device photos and videos, tap Grant access to device photos and allow full access, then tap Start / resume upload and Enable / update automatic sync.

You can later switch to selected folders instead of all device photos. If you choose selected folders, add at least one folder and turn on its cloud switch.

Choose what to sync and upload

All device photos and videos

In Settings → Photo sources, choose All device photos and videos and grant full media access. Use Show device folders and cloud switches to turn upload on or off for individual device folders. Changes apply on the next sync.

Selected folders and subfolders

You do not have to upload every photo on your phone. Choose Selected folders and subfolders in Settings to upload only the phone folders you choose. On the home screen, open Tools (⋮) → Add photo folder to pick one or more folders. Tap Folders at the top to open a separate folder-switch screen. It shows device folders and cloud switches in all-device mode, or your selected folder switches in selected-folder mode. Turn each folder on or off there. When a folder is on, its subfolders are included. A folder with its cloud off is not selected for sync. Tools (⋮) → Clear selected folder choices removes your selected-folder upload list and cloud switches; it does not affect all-device mode; it does not delete photos already on the share.

Start, pause, and resume

Sync means MK-PH checks the enabled photo sources for new files and uploads them to the share. Tap Start / resume upload in Settings for a manual check (up to 10,000 files). Already completed items are skipped. For ongoing checks, choose an interval, tap Save sync interval and then Enable / update automatic sync. Use Disable automatic sync if you want scheduled checks to stop.

Tap Tools (⋮) → Pause upload on the home screen to stop a transfer that is running now, including the current file. Already completed uploads stay on the share. An unfinished temporary upload is removed when the share is reachable; if cleanup cannot connect, a temporary .part file may remain. Tap Start / resume upload when ready. Pause does not disable future automatic checks; choose Disable automatic sync as well if you do not want another scheduled upload.

MK-PH uploads over Wi-Fi by default; mobile data requires the explicit Settings option and an active VPN into the home network. The computer or H-Cloud hosting the share must be on. Android may delay background checks; a 5–14 minute interval runs only while the Settings sync screen stays open. Background intervals start at about 15 minutes.

Browse your photos and videos

The home screen shows this phone's completed uploads grouped by day: each date heading is followed by its photo and video thumbnails. Thumbnails and full-screen items load from the original on this phone, so returning to the gallery does not rescan the H-Cloud . At the top are two rows of four icons: Home, Folders, Browse, Tools (⋮), Photos, Videos, Rename and Delete. Drag the scroll handle on the right to move through dates quickly. Tap Photos or Videos to filter; tap the same icon again for both types. Tools offers All photos and videos, Add photo folder, Clear selected folder choices, Pause upload and Refresh uploaded list. Clear selected folder choices removes only manually picked upload folders; it does not delete media. Use the bottom More menu to manage bookmarks. Videos appear once their uploads finish; the gallery reports how many videos are currently shown.

Tap a thumbnail to open it full-screen. Swipe left or right to go to the next or previous photo, or use the on-screen buttons. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, drag to inspect a zoomed photo, and double-tap to zoom or reset. Tap Back to return. Videos already on this phone play from its local original; media in a shared album downloads from the H-Cloud . Use the playback controls in the viewer. The phone Back button goes to the prior screen; from the main gallery it asks before closing.

Long-press a thumbnail to select it; tap others to select more. The selection bar shows a count such as 1/35 selected and has Select all. Tap More in the bottom bar for selected-item actions. To see bookmarked items, tap More → Bookmarks; MK-PH shows the bookmark count there and clears other gallery filters so your bookmarks can appear. The bottom More (⋮) menu offers Share selected files via apps (sends copies to the chosen Android app), Add to bookmarks, Add to Home screen (one selected item; launcher approval may be needed), and Properties (one item: filename, type, phone model, date, local size and dimensions when available, original phone path, without displaying the backup path). A viewer More menu adds Remove from bookmarks, Bookmarks, Print via an installed app, Open with another app, and About app. Open with hands an app a temporary copy; edits are not automatically saved back to MK-PH or the H-Cloud . Rename changes the backup copy only. Delete asks Android to confirm removal of the phone originals. The option to also delete backup copies is unchecked by default and runs only after phone deletion succeeds. If a selected folder is read-only, delete its originals in the Files app.

Browse opens a date picker with scrollable year, month and day. Choose Show month to see all dates in that month, or Show day for just that date. A date-filtered gallery opens with actual thumbnails, not file paths. Pinch with two fingers on the thumbnail grid to make the tiles larger (fewer columns) or smaller (more columns); your layout choice is saved on this phone.

Create and manage albums

  1. Tap Albums in the bottom bar, then Create album and give it a name.
  2. Return to the gallery. Long-press one photo or video to begin selecting; tap more thumbnails to select a group.
  3. Tap Add to album and choose an album. If none exists yet, MK-PH offers to create an album and adds your selected items. To see an album, open Albums and choose it from the list.

While viewing a local album, long-press an item and select others. Move to another album adds them to another album and removes them from this one. Remove from album only removes them from this album. Delete selected album deletes the album listing, not the original photos or videos. Originals remain in their folders on the H-Cloud , and deleting an album does not delete files from the phone.

Albums you create are stored on this phone unless you explicitly share an album link.

Shared in the bottom bar remembers a household album after you open its code successfully once. Recipients can view it again or remove its shortcut from this phone. The album owner can edit their local album and publish updates, or remove the shared album listing for other phones. Devices that retain SMB access may still read the original files. If a shared item has moved, ask the owner to update the album. Stopping a share removes its metadata, not the original files.

Share photos or an album

Share selected files to another app

Long-press a thumbnail, select the other photos or videos you want, then tap Share files via apps. MK-PH prepares actual copies of the selected files (normally from this phone) and opens Android's sharing menu. Choosing Gmail gives Gmail the files as attachments; any further uploading follows Gmail's behavior. This action is different from sharing an album link.

Share an album with household phones

  1. Open Albums and choose a local album containing the items you want.
  2. Tap Share / update selected album link and send the offered mkph://album/… link.
  3. On another activated phone connected to home Wi-Fi (or your home VPN), use Shared → Add album by code and paste the album code from the message. That phone must have access to the same SMB share.

If you later change the album, tap Share / update selected album link again so other phones see the updated membership. The link opens in MK-PH; it is not a public web gallery. Some messaging or email apps may not make the custom link tappable. MK-PH now includes a plain album code in shared messages. On the receiving phone, tap Shared → Add album by code and paste the code while connected to the home network.

Privacy and local-network security

MK-PH connects directly to the SMB share over your home Wi-Fi or an optional home VPN. For the household setup, the server address is on the private local network; MK-PH does not provide a public website or an internet-facing photo gallery. Its own upload and gallery functions do not send your photos to a cloud service or a third-party server.

The H-Cloud photos are accessible only to people or devices that can reach the share and have a permitted SMB account. Keeping them inaccessible from the public internet also depends on your router and H-Cloud settings: do not forward SMB port 445 to the internet or enable internet-facing access to the share.

Uploads use Wi-Fi by default. Settings has an opt-in switch to allow uploads over mobile data when a VPN into your home network is active. The private H-Cloud address cannot be reached through ordinary mobile data, so keep SMB port 445 off the public internet. If Wi-Fi disconnects and the VPN option is off, an upload stops. When you deliberately tap Share and choose another Android app, that app receives the selected copies or album link and applies its own privacy rules. Only share with apps and people you trust.

After enabling automatic sync during setup, the Settings screen remains open and the applied button stays highlighted. Use Back to gallery when ready.

Settings and this information page

Tap Settings at the bottom of the gallery to change your sources, upload schedule, notifications, share connection or this phone's folder name. Turn on Notify me when new photos are uploaded if you want an upload notification. Change SMB password is for when the Windows or H-Cloud account password changes; it does not change the activation code.

Export encrypted profile can carry household server and sync settings to another phone. Import it there, then provide that phone's SMB password, unique folder name and activation code. The exported profile does not contain the SMB password or this phone's folder permissions.

Tap More → About app to open this guide. MK-PH keeps a copy available offline. The public guide does not show the home share address or folder structure.

If something is not working

  • Nothing uploads: check home Wi-Fi or home VPN, that the share host is on, the SMB account password, Android photo permissions, your selected source and its cloud switch. Start a manual upload to see its status.
  • No gallery items: connect to home Wi-Fi or your home VPN and tap Refresh. Check that the app has completed an upload from this phone.
  • An album link does not open: open the link on a phone with MK-PH activated and access to the same share. Try copying the complete link if your email app does not make it tappable.
  • Automatic checks are late: Android controls background timing. In Settings, confirm automatic sync is enabled and review your phone's battery settings.
